# Rules

1. Cabin work may only be planned in a ground slot that is at least the task
   duration plus 30 minutes of buffer.
2. Work that needs cabin power isolation may not run in parallel with catering
   or cleaning in the same zone.
3. Stations with line maintenance can perform connector and software tasks.
   Rotable LRU replacement requires either base maintenance or an MRO partner
   with the corresponding capability.
4. If the required part is not on stock at the arrival station, the planner must
   compare: expedited freight (surcharge approx. EUR 2,200) against blocking the
   suite for the next legs (approx. EUR 10,000 per leg).
5. A task that would delay departure by more than 20 minutes must be escalated
   to the network control centre. Delay cost is assumed at approx. EUR 95 per
   minute for this exercise.
