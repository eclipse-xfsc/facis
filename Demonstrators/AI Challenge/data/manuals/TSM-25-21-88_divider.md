# Fault code F-25-2188 - Divider drive stall / stow position not reached

## Safety relevance
The privacy divider must be fully stowed for taxi, take-off and landing. A suite
whose divider does not reach the stowed position is NOT sellable and the seat
must be blocked until repair or an approved deferral is in place.

## Procedure
1. Check the divider guide rail for debris and for cushion material trapped in
   the rail.
2. Command a full stow cycle and record the drive current and end position.
   Position error above 10 % confirms the defect.
3. Inspect the drive belt of module DA-DIV-1180 for slip marks.
4. Replace DA-DIV-1180 if the belt is worn or the drive stalls under nominal load.
5. After replacement, perform the cabin-safety functional check and have it
   witnessed by a B1 certifying staff member.
