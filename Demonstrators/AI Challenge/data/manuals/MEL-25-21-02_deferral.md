# Deferral categories used in this exercise

| Item | Condition | Category | Repair interval | Suite sellable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25-21-02a | Backrest actuator inoperative, seat locked upright | C | 10 days | No |
| 25-21-02b | Backrest actuator degraded, full range still reached | D | 120 days | Yes |
| 25-21-02c | Intermittent loss of seat control, reproducible | C | 10 days | No |
| 25-21-02d | Intermittent loss of seat control, not reproducible on ground | C | 10 days | Yes, with cabin log entry and repeat monitoring |
| 25-21-02e | Privacy divider not stowing | A | 1 day | No |
| 23-34-20a | IFE inoperative at one suite | D | 120 days | Yes |
| 25-21-02f | USB-PD port inoperative | D | 120 days | Yes |

A deferral requires: fault code, BITE snapshot, cabin log reference, and the
signature of certifying staff. Category A and C deferrals must be visible to the
network control centre because they constrain the next rotations.
