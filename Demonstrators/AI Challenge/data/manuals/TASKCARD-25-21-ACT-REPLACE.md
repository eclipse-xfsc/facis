# Task card: backrest actuator replacement

Parts: DA-ACT-2201 (1 ea). Consumables: lock wire, grease MIL-spec equivalent.
Tools: torque wrench 2-25 Nm, actuator alignment jig.

## Steps
1. Isolate suite power. Attach the warning tag.
2. Recline the backrest manually to the service position using the mechanical
   release. Do not force the kinematics.
3. Remove the backrest shroud. Six screws, note the two longer screws at the top.
4. Disconnect the actuator connector. Cap the harness end.
5. Remove the two actuator mounting bolts. Support the actuator while removing
   the second bolt.
6. Fit the new actuator with the alignment jig. Torque the bolts to 18 Nm and
   lock-wire them.
7. Reconnect the actuator. Refit the shroud.
8. Restore power. Run 5 full recline cycles and record the current per cycle.
   Acceptance: mean current below 2.8 A, position error below 2 %.
9. Update the component record with the removed and installed serial numbers.
