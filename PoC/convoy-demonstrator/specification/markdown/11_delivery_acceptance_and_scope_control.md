## 11 Delivery, acceptance and scope control
### 11.1 Change control

Scope changes require an explicit client decision and are recorded in the repository change log. An unmet condition precedent of Section 4 is decided in the same way; the decision states its effect on milestone dates and effort.

### 11.2 Client acceptance
Acceptance is recorded by the client on the demonstration protocol once E1–E5 are met, the CI report of the tagged release shows every unit test of Section 10.4 and every unit test named in the acceptance criteria of Sections 5 and 6 passing, the integration tests have passed, every acceptance criterion of Sections 5 and 6 that is verified by inspection (Section 5) is recorded in the protocol without open finding. Ordered options are accepted as stated in Section 2.3. Acceptance is not inferred from silence or from deployment alone.
